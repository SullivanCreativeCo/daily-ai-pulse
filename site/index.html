LI . NO. 02
Two grand to set it up. Five hundred a month to keep it running.

That's the price range Aaron Edwards says works when agencies sell AI customer support agents to local businesses.

The setup fee is the build. It pays you once.

The monthly is the part that matters. The agent answers questions at 2am. A report lands in the owner's inbox. Nobody has to remember anything.

Here's what I like about it. The owner isn't buying AI. Nobody wakes up wanting AI.

They're buying fewer repeat questions. Fewer missed messages. Their Saturday back.

Do more with the same, not the same with less.

That's the pitch. And it's why the price doesn't scare people. Compare it to hiring one part-time person to sit by the phone.

The tech keeps getting cheaper. This week alone, a few more big models announced open weights. The part that doesn't get cheaper is somebody who sets it up right and checks in.

So who's that person in your town?
LI . NO. 03
OpenAI's new agents don't wait for you to type.

They're called dots. Launched at the end of September. A dot keeps working on a goal between conversations and comes back when it needs a decision.

Cool. But that's not the part I'd read twice.

The part I'd read twice is the rules. You set what it does per action. Act on its own, act if pre-approved, ask first, or hand it back to you.

And asking for a draft doesn't mean it can send it.

That's the real question under every AI pitch. Not "can it do the thing." It's "what's it allowed to do without asking me?"

Nobody's scared of the work getting done. They're scared of the email going out at 11pm with their name on it.

So the setup that wins isn't the smartest agent. It's the one with the clearest rules.

Start with drafts only. Earn the sending.

What's the first thing you'd let one send on its own?

AI launches & news

  • Reflection debuts Beam - Jingletree (TechCrunch reporting) Reflection AI says its first open-weight model, Beam, matches Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning. It claims 3-4x less inference compute. It's 501B parameters with 23B active, and has a 1M token context window. Weights are due this month. Nobody has independently checked those numbers yet, so treat them as a claim. The price war on the open side is what to watch. https://www.jingletree.com/reflection-debuts-beam-an-open-weight-ai-model-to-rival-chinese-models-at-lower-compute-cost-282530.html

  • Mistral Large 4 ("Le Chonk") - i10x.ai It's a 1.05T parameter multimodal model with a 1M token context. It's live now as a public preview API, with open weights scheduled later this month. Mistral is selling API access first and releasing weights second. That's a smart way to get paid while everyone benchmarks it. https://i10x.ai/news/mistral-large-4-le-chonk-1-05t-multimodal-model-preview

  • OpenAI dots - Framewright (summarizing OpenAI's Sept 29 launch) Dots are always-on agents inside ChatGPT. One keeps working on a goal between conversations and comes back when it needs a decision. The part worth reading is the control. You set rules per action: act on its own, act if pre-approved, ask first, or hand it back to you. OpenAI also says asking for a draft doesn't authorize sending it. That's the right instinct for anyone putting an agent in a small business's inbox. Pro plans exclude the EEA, UK and Switzerland, so European owners need a business plan. https://framewright.cloud/blog/openai-dots-small-business

  • Durable's AI website builder - Recode You type a business name and industry, and it generates a full site in about 30 seconds. It has since added an AI writer, a CRM and invoicing. The take is that it isn't stealing Squarespace customers. It's catching the owner who would have quit halfway through the template screen. https://recodenews.com/durables-ai-website-builder-is-quietly/

One more thing: a site called World Programming says Meta released Llama 4 Scout. I only found it on that re-post, so I'm not leaning on it until Meta confirms.

Enterprise AI moves

  • ServiceNow AI Workflow Factory - for.you.com It chains Process Mining, Autonomous Engineer, Build Agent and App Engine into one "continuous improvement loop." The loop finds a process worth fixing, builds the fix, and runs it. The diagnosis is the useful bit. Companies don't lack models, they lack a way to turn one pilot win into the next one. Most of the proof so far is ServiceNow's own internal numbers. https://for.you.com/posts/servicenow-ai-workflow-factory-aims-at-agentic-ai-bottleneck-02db4046

  • WSO2 Agent Manager - ForPressRelease This is a control plane for agents: identity, guardrails, tracing, and a one-click suspend. It cites Gartner predicting 150,000 agents in the average Fortune 500 by 2028, with only 13% of orgs feeling governed. "Agent sprawl" is the new shadow IT. If you sell agents to small businesses, "who can shut this off?" is a question you'll get. https://www.forpressrelease.com/forpressrelease/677675/21/wso2-agent-manager-brings-sovereign-ai-governance-to-enterprise-agent-sprawl

New harnesses & tools

  • Paul by Trelium - Y Combinator Launch Paul connects to your email and chat, watches how work moves, and flags repetitive tasks worth automating. It hands you a ready-made prompt to build the agent. It replaces the "AI audit" workshop with something that watches the actual work. Owners can't name their busywork because they've done it so long it's invisible. https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vc0-paul-by-trelium

  • IrisGo - GenZ NewZ You do a task once and it learns the routine ("Watch & Learn"). It also does a daily brief, email triage and meeting summaries. It's free in beta, on-device, and took #1 on Product Hunt Oct 7. The founder is ex-Apple and the seed round was $2.8M led by AI Fund. It's built for solo operators who have too many small chores for any one integration. It's still a beta, so expect rough edges. https://genznewz.com/irisgo-is-the-ai-that-learns-your-job-by-watching

  • EmbeddingGemma 2 - Glonce Google released a 740M open embedding model under Apache 2.0. Text, code, images, video and audio share one embedding space, and it's small enough to run on a phone. If you build search or RAG over a client's files, it runs fully offline. That's an easy privacy pitch for law firms, clinics and anyone nervous about the cloud. https://glonce.com/google-releases-embeddinggemma-2-an-open-740m-multimodal/

  • Codex limits on Pro 200 - OpenAI Developer Community One long-time subscriber says OpenAI's Sept 30 email promised the 20x allowance through Oct 29, with the cut to 10x starting Oct 30. If you build on Codex, check your meter now and plan for Oct 30. https://community.openai.com/t/openai-please-explain-publicly-is-pro-200-codex-usage-being-reduced-before-october-30/1405405
