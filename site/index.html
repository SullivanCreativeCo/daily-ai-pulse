LI . NO. 02
The most interesting line in Sequel's agent launch is the boring one.

Their agents can read and draft on their own. They can't send an email, invite anyone, change an event, or push a lead to the CRM until a person approves.

That's the whole trust model.

Most small business owners aren't scared of AI being dumb. They're scared of it hitting send on something they didn't read.

So the agent does the grunt work. Checks the landing page 72 hours out. Flags the broken CRM sync. Drafts a follow-up that opens with the question the attendee actually asked.

Then a human says yes.

That's not a limitation. That's the feature. It's how you get a cautious owner to say "okay, turn it on."

Do more with the same, not the same with less.

What's one thing in your week you'd let AI draft but never send?
