LI . NO. 02
Gusto just launched an AI teammate called Cofounder, built for small business owners.

Most AI tools make you start from zero. You explain your business, answer ten clarifying questions, then maybe get something useful.

Cofounder shows up already knowing your payroll data, your team roster, your compliance calendar. You say "run payroll Friday, flag anything that needs approval," and it just does it.

Gusto's head of tech put it better than I could. Running payroll was never the hard part. It's everything you have to do ahead of running it. Pulling data from other systems. Chasing missing timesheets. The work before the work.

That phrase is the whole game for small business owners right now.

That's not just a feature. That's a weight removal product.

If you sell to small businesses, the question isn't what task can AI do for them. It's what's sitting in front of the task that they've never had time to name.
