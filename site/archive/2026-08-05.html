Everybody's suddenly scared of their AI bill.

For a minute that felt like maturity. Track your tokens, cap your usage, don't let the meter run wild.

But there's a story going around from inside Meta. One employee used 280 billion tokens in a single month. That's roughly 2.3 million books worth of text. Fifty books a minute, nonstop, for a month.

The company let it happen and called it a leaderboard.

Here's the thing nobody's saying loud enough. The dollar amount was never the real question. The real question is what the task actually produced. A drafted email costs half a cent. Spend wisely, not sparingly. That's the whole shift happening right now.
