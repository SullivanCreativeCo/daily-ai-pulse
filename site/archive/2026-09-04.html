AI launches & news

  • GPT-6 Astra - OpenAI Rolling out in stages. Cybersecurity partners first, then Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise over the next week, then the API and AWS. Free tier users wait longer. It tops FrontierMath Tier 4, saturates ARC-AGI-3, and hits 100% on ExploitBench. Also the first model flagged for "opaque recurrence," a reasoning trick that makes its chain of thought harder for researchers to audit. Convenient timing for a company that just told everyone to trust its alignment work. https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/

"I do think we're there... I think it might be about this model." - Greg Brockman

  • K2 Horizon - Institute of Foundation Models Six open models, 0.9B to 375B parameters, and they didn't just drop the weights. They released the training data, the checkpoints, the actual recipe. This is the one to watch if you build anything. Every closed lab argues "trust our benchmarks." IFM just said here's everything, go check for yourself. That's a different kind of pressure on Anthropic and OpenAI than another chatbot ever could be. https://ifm.ai/blog/k2/

Enterprise AI moves

  • Grok Bot for Enterprise - xAI Free for two weeks, up to 500 seats, zero data retention. Straight shot at Copilot and Gemini's per-seat pricing. Each bot runs in its own sandbox and can message other bots to hand off context. If you're pricing AI tools for a client right now, this changes the floor on what "enterprise AI assistant" costs.

  • Cam - Gearset (Salesforce dev agent) One customer had it writing 44% of their team's pull requests within a week. 75% of those merged clean. Worth noting for anyone selling into dev teams: the pitch wasn't "AI writes your code faster." It was "AI writes code inside your existing review process." Same guardrails, same audit trail. That's the sell that gets past a skeptical engineering lead.

  • OpenClaw 2.0 - open source harness 933 contributors, 16,000 pull requests, a total rebuild after seven quiet weeks. The direction is shared agents, multiple people or bots working the same task together instead of one person babysitting one bot. This is the pattern that's about to show up in every tool you use, whether or not you ever touch OpenClaw itself.
