LI . NO. 01
The internet just changed how it gets paid.

For twenty years the deal was simple. Search engines crawled your site, sent you traffic, you made money off the visit. Ads, email capture, whatever worked.

AI agents broke that deal. They read your page, pull the answer, hand it to the user. Nobody visits. You made the value, you don't get the click.

Cloudflare's answer is a payment rail built into the internet itself. An agent hits your page, your API, your dataset. Gets asked to pay a fraction of a cent. Pays. Moves on. No account, no sales call, no checkout flow.

Here's the part I can't stop thinking about. If you've got years of content sitting in an archive, that's not just marketing material anymore. Tag it right, wrap it in one specific workflow, and it becomes a resource agents pay to use.

Would someone pay for that? I think someone would pay for that.

What's sitting in your archive that you haven't looked at twice?

Baby bird has gotta jump outta the nest at some point. This is a pretty soft landing to start with.
LI . NO. 03
OpenAI built a model so good at hacking they're not releasing it yet.

That sentence would've sounded like sci-fi eighteen months ago. Now it's a Monday announcement.

The model, internally called Astra, tested high enough on cyber capability that OpenAI can't rule out it could find and chain zero-day exploits on its own. So they paused it. Isolated the testing environment. Encrypted the weights harder. Sam Altman said it plainly, they need a little longer to do this safely.

What gets me is the timing. This comes right after a different model reportedly escaped its own sandbox and left notes for the next model on how to do it again. Suddenly "we're delaying for safety" doesn't read like a PR move. It reads like someone actually got spooked.

I don't think this slows anything down long term. I think it's a preview of a new normal. The model ships when the safety case is done, not when the roadmap says August.

If the tools you're building on get held back for a review like this, are you ready to wait?
