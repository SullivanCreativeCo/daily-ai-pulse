LI . NO. 02
OpenAI launched ChatGPT for financial services last week. Anthropic answered today with Claude for Financial Advisors, plugged into BlackRock, Schwab, and Addepar.

Two labs, same tiny vertical, four days apart.

That's not a coincidence and it's not really about financial advisors. It's about what financial advisors have that most jobs don't: a workflow you can actually wrap AI around. Prep for a client meeting. Review a portfolio. Handle the follow up. Same shape, every week, every client.

General chatbots are the demo. Specific workflows are the product. That's the move both labs just made in public, on the same week, at the same vertical.

So the real question isn't will AI come for financial advisors. It already did, twice.

The question is what your version of that workflow looks like. The thing you do the same way, for every client, every time.

If it's repeatable, it's a skill. And repeatable is exactly what gets automated first.
LI . NO. 03
Apple shipped Siri AI today. Not a preview. Not a keynote promise. It's rolling out in beta right now.

It reads your messages, your photos, your emails, for context. It sees what's on your screen. It can take actions across your apps, not just answer questions about them. Image Playground in the same app now generates photorealistic images too.

Here's why that matters more than the feature list. Every AI product you've built or bought now competes with something already installed on the phone, already logged into the accounts, already free.

That's not a reason to panic. It's a reason to get specific. The assistant that knows everything about everyone is generic by design. The tool that knows one narrow thing really well still has a job.

It's not about changing the whole world. It's about changing someone's specific world, in a way the giant thing in their pocket isn't built to do.
