LI . NO. 03
Elizabeth Field DiGiovanni runs a travel activity box company by herself. Two kids under five. Factories in Asia that are awake while she's asleep.

A supplier quote used to take her three weeks. Every question cost a day.

Now she writes up what she needs before bed. Product, target cost, certifications, quantity. Agents work the suppliers overnight. She reads what came back before the kids are up and makes the call.

In her words: "You don't need a bigger team. You need the night shift covered."

Look at what she kept. She sets the target cost. She picks the supplier. She decides which certification claim needs a document behind it.

The agent does the waiting. She does the deciding.

Same pattern showing up all over the news this week. Agents prep and check. Humans approve the orders, the payments, the merge.

Which part of your week is just waiting, and which part actually needs you?

AI launches & news

  • Gemini 4 Neon - The Hindu, Startup Ideas Podcast Google's mid-tier model for complex coding, analysis and long agentic work. Carbon is the heavy one, Hydrogen is fast and everyday, and Neon sits between them. The podcast's tips: set thinking to high, build in Antigravity instead of a bare wrapper, and give it a verification loop so it opens the browser and fixes its own bugs. Two honest caveats from that episode. Neon runs brief, and it leans on em dashes and "it's not X, it's Y" phrasing. https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/technology/google-launches-gemini-4-neon-taking-on-claude-opus-55-and-gpt-62-sol/article71727599.ece

  • Inkling 2 Mini - Thinking Machines Lab 26.4B total parameters, 3.4B active, about 20GB in memory. It runs on a laptop, open weights. It's a looped transformer: the middle layers run three times per token, so it reasons more without writing every step down. They say it leads open models under 100B on agentic coding benchmarks. The team ran it offline on a MacBook Pro during a flight and had it build a playable flight simulator in a few minutes. It's weaker at multi-turn chat and instructions with lots of constraints, and knowledge doesn't improve with looping. https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/

  • Tencent Hy4 full open weights - DeAI 770B total, 49B active, 1M context, with an 82-page system card. Big, but read the license before the benchmarks. The August preview was Apache 2.0. This one carries a clause requiring a separate written license from Tencent if you run a model-as-a-service business above $50M gross revenue or ship to 100M monthly users. Fine-tunes and distillations inherit it. Every benchmark number is Tencent's own. You rent hardware for this one, you don't run it at a desk. https://www.deai.org/news/tencent-hy4-full-open-weights-system-card

  • ChatGPT Space reaches Plus - Tom's Guide The $20 plan now gets documents that update themselves from connected apps like Gmail and Slack, plus editable slide decks with real charts you can export to PowerPoint. Example prompt from the piece: a weekly dashboard that refreshes every Monday morning from your connected apps. https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/chatgpt/chatgpt-just-got-a-massive-upgrade-it-can-now-create-documents-that-update-themselves

  • OpenAI on adversarial distillation - OpenAI Their worry is people copying a frontier model's protected reasoning to train a student model that has none of the safeguards. They say attack success against GPT-6.2 Astra dropped from 54.0% to 0.2% in internal red-teaming. They also say they're enforcing against campaigns of thousands to tens of thousands of accounts weekly. That's self-reported. https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation/

Enterprise AI moves

  • Hermes Enterprise on Microsoft Foundry - Nous Research Each agent gets its own work account: email, a OneDrive, a calendar, a Teams profile and a manager. IT approves one blueprint, and every copy an employee creates inherits the policy. The agent reads only its own inbox and edits only what people share with it. Block the blueprint and every copy stops. It's in preview. The useful idea is treating an agent like a new hire instead of a mystery integration. Small businesses can steal that mental model for free. https://nousresearch.com/hermes-enterprise-on-microsoft-foundry

  • AWS Quick update - AWS Describe a process in chat and it builds a flow. Several specialized agents work on one request and Quick merges the answer. Work keeps running after you close the tab.   • Superhuman Go goes proactive - Superhuman Go looks across your tools, finds what needs attention and suggests the next move. You approve what gets done, sent or shared.
