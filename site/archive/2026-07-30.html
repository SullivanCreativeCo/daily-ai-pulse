Today's headline

MoonPay gave AI agents a wallet.

Their new tool PayBox connects to Claude or ChatGPT and lets your agent actually move money. You describe what you want, it preps the transaction, you approve with a passkey, the money moves.

We've been talking about AI agents "doing things" for two years now. This is the first time one can pay for something without you opening a new tab.
