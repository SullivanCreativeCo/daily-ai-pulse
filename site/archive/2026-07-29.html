That's not an AI problem. That's an ownership problem wearing an AI costume.

The businesses that win this year aren't the ones with the fanciest agent. They're the ones who can answer "what's running and who owns it" without opening five different tools to find out.

How many automations are running in your business right now that only one person actually understands?
