LI . NO. 02
Corey Ganim posted the exact numbers behind a $10K a month AI services business. Free assessment, paid assessment, retainer.

Why that order matters is the part people skip.

The free call isn't free because you're generous. It's free because you don't have proof yet. You do one to three of them, get a testimonial, and stop.

The paid assessment isn't the moneymaker either. It's the trust builder. Five hundred to two grand, three to seven fixes, a real report. It's priced low enough to be a no-brainer yes.

The retainer is where the actual business lives. Two calls a month, one process automated at a time.

Most people skip straight to trying to sell the retainer. Nobody buys a monthly commitment from a stranger. They buy it from someone who already fixed one small thing for them.

Would someone pay for the small fix first? I think someone would.

AI launches & news

  • Grok Bot - xAI (now SpaceXAI) Persistent agents with their own dedicated cloud machine. They sign into apps that don't even have APIs, remember how you like things done, and only interrupt when they need approval. Available now for SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra, and Cursor Teams subscribers, $120 to $300 a month depending on the plan. https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot

  • Symphony - Wix A multi-agent system built specifically for small business ops, not website building. There's an orchestrator called Maestro that runs a "morning standup," summarizes what got done overnight and flags what needs your eyes. Wix is leaning hard on the fact they've watched hundreds of millions of small businesses operate. That's a real edge if they use it right. https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/11/wix-launches-symphony-new-standalone-multi-agent-system-built-business-operations/

  • Qwen3.8-Max goes GA - Alibaba Real benchmarks, real pricing, live API. But the open weights they promised for "the week of August 10" are still nowhere. Worth watching because Alibaba's whole pitch was being first to open-source a flagship-tier model. A late promise undercuts the story they're trying to tell. https://awesomeagents.ai/models/qwen-3-8-max/

Enterprise AI moves

  • Gemini app hits 1 billion monthly users - Google Fastest growing product in Google's history. 63% of users now just talk to it. One in five Gemini Live sessions use the camera, people pointing their phone at stuff and asking questions in real time. Small businesses are apparently leaning on it for creative work too, 150 million images a day generated inside it. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/one-billion-monthly-users/

  • 41 Stats About AI Adoption - AI Daily Brief (NLW) The number that stuck with me: 98% of C-suite leaders say token costs are making them rethink their AI plans. Only 64% of them actually meter their usage. So almost everyone's worried about the bill and less than two thirds of them are even watching the meter. That gap is the whole game right now. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Laws8go7v98

  • Nemotron 3.5 Lightning + NeMo Switchyard - NVIDIA A small fast model plus an open router that sends each step of an agent's work to whichever model actually needs to handle it. The point is you stop paying frontier-model prices for a tool call that a $3-active-parameter model can do just as well. This is the "stop burning tokens on busywork" move, and it's going to matter a lot once everyone's running agents 24/7 like Grok Bot. https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-ai-releases-nemotron-3-5-lightning-and-nemo-switchyard/

New harnesses & tools

  • Muse Glimmer - Meta Superintelligence Labs A 30B model, weights open under Apache 2.0, that runs on a single consumer GPU with no internet required. Built for local agents and function calling. If you've ever had an automation die because wifi dropped, this is the answer to that. https://www.verdict.co.uk/meta-superintelligence-labs-muse-glimmer/

  • Muse Code - Meta Meta's first real coding agent. Cheaper tokens if you let them train on your prompts, full price if you don't. No independent benchmark backing up the performance claims yet. Worth knowing the tradeoff exists before you plug it into a client codebase. https://tech-insider.org/ie/meta-muse-code-launch-2026/
