LI . NO. 03
If it's repeatable, it's a skill.

Heard a guy explain this on a podcast today and it clicked in a way most AI advice doesn't. He runs his whole company on Claude Code skills, basically SOPs written down as a document the AI reads before it does the task.

Without one, every time you ask for a proposal you're re-explaining your preferences from scratch. Where the price goes, what colors, what tone. You land on something good, then next week you start over.

With a skill, he typed one line, "create a proposal for this client," and got the exact branded doc he wanted. First try. Nothing re-explained.

The part that actually matters isn't the tool. It's the habit underneath it. Any time you catch yourself explaining the same thing to an AI twice, that's the moment to write it down once and stop.
