Today's headline

Anthropic and OpenAI both dropped new models on the same day this week.

That basically never happens. Usually one lab ships early so they don't get drowned out by the other.

The part worth sitting with isn't who "won." It's that Opus 5.5 is the first model in a while people are actually excited about. After a couple of rough releases, Anthropic clawed the room back. That's rare in this space. Most labs ship and move on. This one looked like they actually listened to the feedback.

Meta just opened the door and most people are going to miss it.

Back in 2008, Apple opened the App Store. Outside developers could build something useful for people who already owned an iPhone and get paid for it. Two years in, Apple had paid developers a billion dollars.

Meta just did the same thing with Muse, their AI agent. It's called connectors. Any business can plug into Muse, so when someone asks it to book a room or buy something, your service can be the one that answers.

1,500 developers applied in under a week. Stripe, Shopify, Best Buy and Sephora are already wired in.

Here's the part I keep coming back to. The opportunity isn't the big brands. It's the small, specific service that shows up at exactly the right moment in someone's request. A paddle court with an open slot Tuesday night. A studio with two free hours.

Would someone pay for that? I think someone would pay for that.

What's the version of that for your business?
LI . NO. 02
Anthropic shipped a model nobody liked, then fixed it.

Opus 5 landed and people were not into it. Not politely lukewarm, genuinely annoyed. Then this week Opus 5.5 came out and the same people are calling it a comeback.

Someone made a meme for it. A horse drawn beautifully, then scribbled into a mess for the bad versions, then back to a clean, detailed horse for 5.5. That's basically the whole story in one image.

Nobody talks about the version that flopped first. We only see the fixed one and call it genius.

Your ideas aren't bad ideas. They're just not finished ideas. Anthropic didn't scrap Opus and start over. They took the feedback and kept building the same thing until it worked.

What are you sitting on right now that just needs one more pass?
