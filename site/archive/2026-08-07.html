Today's headline

A standard just dropped for packaging AI skills and MCP servers.

Sounds boring. It's not.

Right now if you build a skill for one client, you rebuild the wrapper for the next one. Different manifest, different folder shape, same skill underneath.

Agent Plugins 1.0 fixes that. Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Vercel wrote it. Google joined as a maintainer today. Package once, ship to ChatGPT, Cursor, Copilot, VS Code, and Kiro without redoing the wrapper every time.

Every AI concierge builder hits the same wall eventually.

You build a great skill for one client. Next client, you're rebuilding the wrapper. Different manifest, different folder shape, same skill underneath.

Today Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Vercel published a standard for this. Google just joined. It's called Agent Plugins.

LI . NO. 03
OpenAI just shipped an update that sounds small and isn't.

GPT-5.6 now offers a correction when just agreeing with you wouldn't actually help.

That's it. A model that's allowed to say "actually, no" instead of nodding along to whatever you typed.

Most of the AI tools small business owners lean on right now are built to please. Ask if your idea is good and it'll find a way to say yes.

A model that pushes back a little is worth more than one with a bigger context window. Nobody builds a business plan they can trust off a yes-man.

Who's the person in your life that tells you the truth instead of what you want to hear? Your AI should work the same way.
