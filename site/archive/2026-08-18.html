LI . NO. 03
Most people are using AI agents like a search bar with extra steps.

That's not what makes them useful.

A guy on the Startup Ideas Podcast broke down setting up Claude Code the same way you'd onboard a new hire. Give it a workspace. Give it memory, the actual context on what you're building and who it's for. A brief before it touches anything. A real ticket, not a vague "make this better." Eyes, so it can open the app and click through like a real user would. A review step. A schedule for recurring work. Permissions, so it knows what it can decide alone and what needs you.

Seven things. Same seven things a new hire needs on day one.

Once that's in place, the tool stops feeling like a chat window and starts feeling like someone actually working for you at 2am.

What would change if the AI running in the background of your business had a real job description instead of a prompt box?
